About these examples

These are curated English and Chinese voice-cloning examples. They illustrate speech realism and expression; they do not cover all 23 supported languages or represent a blind preference test. See the model card for language coverage and evaluation.

Sizes use the projects’ published model-size conventions: Audio8 Realistic Air and Preview 0.6B, Breeze-TTS-2 ~3B, Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base 1.7B, and Higgs TTS 3 ~4B. Counting conventions differ; Audio8’s 0.6B excludes its separate audio codec.
